Case Study

VCT Sync

A mobile experience designed for Valorant Champions Tour fans who want structured match insights without clutter or distraction.

Overview

VCT Sync is a mobile app concept designed for Valorant Champions Tour fans who want fast, focused updates without clutter or distraction. The app combines live scores, brackets, team updates, and player statistics into a clean, spoiler free experience built for clarity and speed.

1. Empathize

I explored the friction fans experience when using current esports tracking apps. Through informal interviews and observation, three major pain points emerged.

  • Intrusive ads. Users described ads as disruptive and frustrating. They interrupt browsing and make the experience feel cheap.
  • Navigation frustration. Fans struggle to quickly find match stats, brackets, and team information. Many rely on workarounds.
  • Information overload. Users want fast updates, but current apps overwhelm them with dense layouts and excessive detail.

Interview insights

  • Fans care about the story of the match, not just the final score.
  • Statistics alone do not explain what mattered in the game.
  • Users want to feel connected while viewing matches through chat and shared reactions.

2. Define

People problem: Fans often miss important matches due to busy schedules. When they return, they want concise but meaningful insight into what happened, not just raw numbers.

  • Stats without context. High ACS or KDA does not always capture turning points or clutch plays.
  • Ads as deal breakers. Poor ad placement disrupts flow and causes users to uninstall.
  • Cluttered layouts. Wiki style pages make it difficult to quickly check results or upcoming matches.

Target audience: Esports fans ages 15 to 30 who follow VCT and want meaningful insights they can apply to their own gameplay.

Goal: Surface pivotal in game moments in a digestible format so users can quickly understand how a match unfolded and why it mattered.

3. Ideate

Based on research insights, I brainstormed with peers to explore how a cleaner, more focused esports experience could be designed. Ideas were grouped into key themes using FigJam.

  • Layout simplicity. Reduce visual clutter and prioritize essential information.
  • Smarter stat displays. Highlight pivotal plays and momentum shifts instead of only raw numbers.
  • Respectful ad experience. Consider non intrusive monetization models that preserve user focus.
  • Community connection. Integrate lightweight chat or reactions to foster shared viewing experiences.

4. Prototype

I created an interactive prototype that reflects the full user journey, from logging in and selecting favorite teams to exploring match summaries and pivotal moments.

  • Live match dashboard with clear score hierarchy
  • Momentum timeline highlighting turning points
  • Clean bracket view optimized for mobile
  • Spoiler free navigation options
  • Community interaction layer for fan engagement

The final design balances speed, clarity, and emotional storytelling to help fans stay connected without feeling overwhelmed.
