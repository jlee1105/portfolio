Case Study

VCT Sync

A mobile experience designed for Valorant Champions Tour fans who want structured match insights without clutter or distraction.

Overview

VCT Sync is a mobile app concept designed for Valorant Champions Tour fans who want fast, focused updates without clutter or distraction. The app combines live scores, brackets, team updates, and player statistics into a clean, spoiler free experience built for clarity and speed.

1. Empathize

Through informal interviews and observation, three major pain points emerged.

  • Intrusive ads. Users described ads as disruptive.
  • Navigation frustration. Fans struggle to find key information quickly.
  • Information overload. Current apps overwhelm users.

Interview insights

  • Fans care about the story of the match.
  • Statistics alone do not explain impact.
  • Users want to feel connected while viewing.

2. Define

Fans often miss matches and want concise but meaningful insight when they return.

  • Stats lack context.
  • Ads disrupt flow.
  • Clutter slows users.

3. Ideate

Ideas focused on clarity, momentum storytelling, and reducing clutter.

  • Layout simplicity
  • Smarter stat displays
  • Respectful ad experience
  • Community connection
